Orthodontic Braces

Orthodontic braces remain the clinical gold standard for correcting severe malocclusion, irregular crowding, open bites, and jaw discrepancies in children, teenagers, and adults alike.

  • ✓ Metal and aesthetic ceramic bracket options
  • ✓ Corrects chewing efficiency and protects joints (TMJ)
  • ✓ Monthly adjustments with clear progress monitoring

Clear Aligners

Clear aligners are custom-made transparent plastic trays that fit snugly over your teeth. They gradually exert gentle pressure to align teeth without noticeable brackets or dietary restrictions.

  • ✓ Completely removable for meals, brushing, and special occasions
  • ✓ Virtually invisible appearance ideal for working professionals
  • ✓ Smooth, comfortable medical-grade plastic without wire irritation

Dental Implants

Dental implants replace both the root and the visible crown of missing teeth. An osseointegrated titanium post bonds firmly into the jawbone, preserving surrounding facial structure and bone density.

  • ✓ Natural chewing force identical to biological teeth
  • ✓ No need to grind down neighboring healthy teeth
  • ✓ Long-lasting solution that prevents jawbone resorption

Custom Dentures

For extensive or full tooth loss, custom complete and partial dentures restore chewing ability, smile symmetry, and lip fullness with careful focus on mucosal comfort and secure retention.

  • ✓ Complete and cast partial denture prosthetics
  • ✓ Flexible acrylic bases for improved gum tolerance
  • ✓ Dedicated post-delivery adjustments for bite balance

Veneers & Crowns

Dental veneers and full ceramic crowns restore worn, discolored, or root canal-treated teeth. Ceramic materials reflect light identically to natural enamel for seamless aesthetic harmony.

  • ✓ Ultra-thin porcelain veneers to correct spacing & chips
  • ✓ High-strength monolithic zirconia and E-max crowns
  • ✓ Custom shade matching against natural adjacent dentition
