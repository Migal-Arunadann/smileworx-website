Locally Rooted Healthcare

Serving Families in Ponniammanmedu & North Chennai

Located at #12/C, Valliammai Nagar Extension in Ponniammanmedu, Smileworx Dental Care provides neighborhood residents and surrounding North Chennai communities with accessible, gentle dental treatments. Whether you need routine preventive advice, orthodontic correction, or permanent implants, our practice approaches each clinical challenge with scientific care and human warmth.

Our consultations are intentionally unhurried. Dental anxieties are natural, and we recognize that patients often postpone visits when past treatments felt rushed or unclear. At Smileworx, we explain the reasons behind every clinical finding, detail procedural steps beforehand, and review maintenance protocols so you always feel completely in control.

Clinical Safety

Hospital-Grade Hygiene & Sterilization Standards

We strictly enforce multi-stage infection prevention protocols for every single patient appointment.

01

Autoclave Sterilization

All reusable dental instruments undergo pressurized, high-temperature medical autoclave cycles between every patient.

02

Disposable Disposables

Single-use suction tips, gloves, patient drapes, and sterilized examination mirrors are discarded immediately after each use.

03

Operatory Sanitization

Dental chairs and high-touch surface zones are treated with clinical-grade surface disinfectants after every consultation.
